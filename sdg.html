SDG & Impact

How FoodRescue connects to global goals and real data.

Goal One

SDG 2 — Zero Hunger

  • SDG 2 aims to end hunger everywhere.
  • It focuses on food security for all people.
  • It focuses on improved nutrition.
  • FoodRescue contributes by helping redistribute surplus food to people who need it.
Goal Two

SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production

  • Food waste is an inefficient use of resources.
  • Usable food can be redistributed instead of discarded.
  • Responsible consumption includes reducing unnecessary waste.

FoodRescue supports SDG 12 by encouraging people to redistribute usable surplus food.
